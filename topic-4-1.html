Topic 4.1

Technological Innovations from 1450 to 1750

Learning objectives

  • Explain how cross-cultural interactions resulted in the diffusion of technology and facilitated changes in patterns of trade and travel from 1450 to 1750.
Progress 0% · Score 0

European mariners combined older Afro-Eurasian technologies: the magnetic compass and sternpost rudder (China), lateen sails (Indian Ocean), astrolabe, and new cartography. The Portuguese caravel and later carrack/galleon made oceanic voyages more viable.

Gunpowder weapons and navigational knowledge let states sponsor transoceanic exploration. Innovation was cross-cultural, not a purely European invention story.
