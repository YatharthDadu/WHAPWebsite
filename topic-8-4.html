The Chinese Communist Party under Mao won the civil war in 1949, promising land reform and national independence after Japanese invasion and Nationalist weakness. The Great Leap Forward caused catastrophic famine; the Cultural Revolution attacked perceived enemies and disrupted education.

Land reform and nationalization also occurred in other socialist states and in non-communist populist programs (Mexico’s earlier Cárdenas oil nationalization; later land reforms). Communist parties fought in Vietnam and elsewhere as anti-colonial and social revolutions overlapped.
