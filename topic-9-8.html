Topic 9.8

Continuity and Change in a Globalized World

Learning objectives

  • Explain how and why globalization changed international interactions over time.
Progress 0% · Score 0

People, goods, money, and information move faster than in 1900, yet states, religions, and inequalities persist. International organizations (UN, WTO, WHO, regional unions) exist alongside renewed great-power rivalry. The 20th century’s mass violence and decolonization remade the map; the 21st faces climate, pandemics, and digital networks that no one state fully controls.

For the AP exam, argue with specifics: what continued (state sovereignty, economic inequality, cultural diversity) and what changed (speed of connection, legal human-rights talk, environmental scale).
