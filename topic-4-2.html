Topic 4.2

Exploration: Causes and Events from 1450 to 1750

Learning objectives

  • Explain the economic causes and effects of maritime exploration by the various European states.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Portuguese and Spanish monarchs sponsored voyages to reach Indian Ocean wealth, spread Christianity, and compete with rivals after Ottoman control of eastern Mediterranean routes raised the cost of spices. Prince Henry’s patronage, Vasco da Gama’s arrival in India (1498), Columbus (1492), and Magellan’s circumnavigation opened a new map of power.

Northern Europeans (Dutch, English, French) followed with joint-stock companies. China and Japan restricted most maritime outreach; Zheng He’s earlier voyages had already stopped. European exploration was state-sponsored competition, not the only seafaring tradition on earth.
