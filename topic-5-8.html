Topic 5.8

Reactions to the Industrial Economy from 1750 to 1900

Learning objectives

  • Explain the causes and effects of calls for changes in industrial societies from 1750 to 1900.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Labor unions organized for wages, hours, and safety. Socialists and Marxists argued that industrial capitalism exploited workers; The Communist Manifesto (1848) called for proletarian revolution. Utopian socialists offered cooperative alternatives.

Governments sometimes reformed (Britain’s factory acts, later social insurance in Germany under Bismarck — to undercut socialism). Suffrage expanded unevenly. Some conservatives and Romantics rejected industrial ugliness. In colonial settings, workers and peasants faced cash taxes and plantation discipline.
