Topic 6.1

Rationales for Imperialism from 1750 to 1900

Learning objectives

  • Explain how ideologies contributed to the development of imperialism from 1750 to 1900.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Imperialists justified expansion with nationalism, the ‘civilizing mission’ (mission civilisatrice, White Man’s Burden), Christian missionary work, and Social Darwinist claims that some races were fated to rule. Economic arguments (markets, investment, raw materials) mixed with strategic rivalry among Great Powers.

These ideologies masked violence and were contested by critics, including some liberals, socialists, and later anti-colonial nationalists. Scientific racism misused biology to rank peoples.
