Causes of World War I

Learning objectives

  • Explain the causes and consequences of World War I.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Militarism, alliance systems (Triple Alliance vs. Triple Entente), imperialism, and nationalism created a powder keg. The assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand in Sarajevo triggered alliance commitments. Industrial war plans (timetables, railways) made last-minute diplomacy hard.

This was a global war from the start because empires dragged in colonies and extra-European theaters. Historians debate Germany’s particular responsibility versus a shared systemic crisis.
