Topic 9.6

Globalized Culture After 1900

Learning objectives

  • Explain how and why globalization changed culture over time.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Hollywood, Bollywood, K-pop, reggae, football (soccer), and the Olympics created shared references. Consumer brands and English as a lingua franca spread. At the same time, people remixed global forms with local meaning — Bollywood is not a copy of Hollywood; Pentecostal Christianity grew with local styles; fusion cuisines multiplied.

UNESCO and heritage movements tried to protect local traditions. Migration and the internet intensified both homogenization fears and creative hybridity.
