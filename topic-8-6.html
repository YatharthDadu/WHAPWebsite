Topic 8.6

Newly Independent States

Learning objectives

  • Explain how political changes in the period from c. 1900 to the present led to territorial, demographic, and nationalist developments.
  • Explain the economic changes and continuities resulting from the process of decolonization.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Partition and new borders produced refugee crises (India/Pakistan, Israel/Palestine). Some states tried import-substitution industrialization; others remained commodity exporters. Authoritarian one-party or military regimes were common as leaders prioritized unity and development.

Israel’s creation (1948) and subsequent wars reshaped the Middle East. Egypt under Nasser nationalized the Suez Canal (1956). Non-alignment and pan-African/pan-Arab ideas competed with old imperial ties and new superpower aid. Economic challenges included debt, weak infrastructure, and elite capture.
