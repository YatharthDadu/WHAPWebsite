Topic 2.4

Trans-Saharan Trade Routes

Learning objectives

  • Explain the causes and effects of the growth of trans-Saharan trade.
Progress 0% · Score 0

The camel saddle and caravan organization enabled regular crossing of the Sahara. West African gold, enslaved people, and kola moved north; salt, horses, and manufactured goods moved south. Islam traveled with the caravans.

Mali’s rulers taxed trade and displayed wealth (Mansa Musa). Cities such as Timbuktu and Gao became both commercial and scholarly centers. Expanding empires (Mali, later Songhai) both caused and resulted from trade growth.
