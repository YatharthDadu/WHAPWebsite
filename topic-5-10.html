Continuity and Change in the Industrial Age

Learning objectives

  • Explain the extent to which industrialization brought change from 1750 to 1900.

Industry remade energy, war, cities, and the global division of labor. It did not instantly erase peasant majorities, religious life, or dynastic empires. Japan showed non-Western industrial takeoff; Qing China and the Ottomans struggled to match it. The industrial core’s appetite for materials and markets set the stage for imperial scramble (Unit 6).
