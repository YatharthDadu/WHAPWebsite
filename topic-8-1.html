Topic 8.1

Setting the Stage for the Cold War and Decolonization

Learning objectives

  • Explain the historical context of the Cold War after 1945.
Progress 0% · Score 0

The United States and the Soviet Union emerged from WWII with unmatched military power, opposing ideologies (liberal capitalism vs. Marxist-Leninist one-party socialism), and competing occupation zones in Europe. Anti-imperial movements, many of which had fought in or been radicalized by the world wars, demanded independence. The UN offered a new forum even as it reflected great-power vetoes.

Europe was devastated; Britain and France could not easily hold empires. The atomic bomb and soon the Soviet bomb made direct superpower war uniquely dangerous — conflict shifted toward proxies, aid, and ideology.
