Topic 6.7

Effects of Migration

Learning objectives

  • Explain how and why new patterns of migration affected society from 1750 to 1900.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Migrant ethnic enclaves (Chinese in Southeast Asia and the Americas, Indians in the Caribbean and Africa, Italians in Argentina and the U.S., Lebanese in the Americas) preserved language and networks. Male-majority labor migration reshaped gender ratios at home and abroad.

Receiving societies often reacted with nativism and racial exclusion (Chinese Exclusion Act, White Australia policy). Remittances and return migration linked homelands to new economies. Cities became more culturally mixed — and more unequal.
