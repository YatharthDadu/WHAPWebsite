Portugal built a trading-post empire in Africa and Asia (Goa, Melaka, Macao). Spain claimed vast American territories and the Philippines. The Dutch VOC and English East India Company mixed private capital and state charters. France settled Canada and Caribbean islands.

Africa: Asante and Kongo interacted with European trade, including the slave trade. Asia: many states (Tokugawa Japan, Ming/Qing China) set terms for limited European access rather than being fully colonized in this period.
