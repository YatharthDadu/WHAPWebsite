# Tapestry — AP World History Interactive Course

Welcome to **Tapestry**, a comprehensive and interactive learning platform designed specifically for AP World History: Modern. Our goal is to provide a rich, visually engaging, and highly organized way for students to navigate through the expansive tapestry of world history.

Tapestry brings the AP World History Course and Exam Description (CED) to life. It breaks down the curriculum into an intuitive, hierarchical structure that makes studying more manageable and far more engaging. No complex installations or account sign-ups are required—simply open `index.html` in your favorite web browser and start exploring!

## 🌟 Features

* **Intuitive Navigation:** A streamlined hierarchical structure guiding you from the broadest eras down to specific historical topics.
  * **4 Time Periods** covering the entirety of the AP World History: Modern curriculum.
  * **9 Core Units** providing thematic focus and context.
  * **70 Topic Timelines** detailing critical historical events, concepts, and developments.
* **Checkpoint Quizzes:** Built-in assessments at the end of topics and units to test comprehension and reinforce learning.
* **Persistent Progress:** Your quiz progress and checkpoint completions are automatically saved locally in your browser using `localStorage`. You can pick up right where you left off.
* **Dark Mode Support:** A built-in theme toggle to switch between light and dark modes for a comfortable reading experience at any time of day.
* **No Account Required:** Completely localized and browser-based, ensuring absolute privacy and seamless access without the need for logins.
* **Responsive Design:** A beautifully styled, modern interface that works perfectly across different screen sizes.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Clone or Download the Repository:** Obtain a copy of the Tapestry codebase to your local machine.
2. **Open the Course:** Navigate to the root directory and open `index.html` in any modern web browser (e.g., Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox).
3. **Explore the Eras:** Start at the Home page to get an overview of the 4 Time Periods.
4. **Dive into Topics:** Click through a Period to view its Units, and select a Unit to access the detailed Topic Timelines.
5. **Test Your Knowledge:** At the end of a topic or unit, take the Checkpoint Quiz to evaluate your understanding of the material.
6. **Track Your Progress:** Your progress is saved automatically within your browser!

## 📂 Project Structure

The project has been organized to ensure easy maintainability and clear separation of concerns:

* `/` (Root): Contains the main `index.html` entry point.
* `/periods/`: HTML files corresponding to the 4 main AP World History time periods.
* `/units/`: HTML files for the 9 core units.
* `/topics/`: Detailed HTML files for all 70 specific topic timelines.
* `/css/`: Stylesheets for the application.
* `/js/`: JavaScript logic for interactivity, theme toggling, and saving progress.
* `/assets/`: Images and icons used throughout the course.
* `/docs/`: Additional documentation and original notes (e.g., WHAPNotes.pdf).

## 🎓 About

Tapestry is designed primarily for classroom use and individual study, meticulously aligned to the AP World History: Modern Course and Exam Description (CED). Whether you're an educator looking for an interactive tool to engage your students, or a student preparing for the big exam, Tapestry weaves the complex threads of history into a clear, compelling narrative.
