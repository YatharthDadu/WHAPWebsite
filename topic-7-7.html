WWII was even more total: Blitzkrieg, strategic bombing, aircraft carriers, tanks, and the atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki. The Eastern Front and the war in China produced staggering deaths. The Holocaust (Unit 7.8) was industrialized genocide.

Allied coalition (U.S., USSR, Britain, China, and others) out-produced the Axis. Governments mobilized women (U.S. factories; Soviet combat roles). Firebombing of cities erased the civilian/military line. Colonial empires fought again, accelerating later independence.
