Topic 9.1

Advances in Technology and Exchange After 1900

Learning objectives

  • Explain how the development of new technologies changed the world from 1900 to present.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Petroleum, electricity, and later nuclear power remade energy. Antibiotics, vaccines, and sanitation raised life expectancy even as new diseases emerged. Radio, television, and the internet compressed distance. Air travel and container shipping accelerated globalization of goods and people.

Green Revolution agriculture (high-yield grains, fertilizers) increased food supply, with environmental costs. Digital networks created new industries and new inequalities of access.
