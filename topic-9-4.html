Topic 9.4

Economics in the Global Age

Learning objectives

  • Explain the continuities and changes in the global economy from 1900 to present.
Progress 0% · Score 0

After 1980 many states moved toward more market-oriented policies: China’s reform and opening (Deng), India’s 1991 liberalization, post-Soviet shock therapy, and the Washington Consensus in parts of Latin America and Africa. Multinational corporations, the WTO, IMF, and World Bank structured trade and finance. Knowledge and service work grew in some cores; manufacturing shifted to East and Southeast Asia.

Regional blocs (EU, NAFTA/USMCA, ASEAN) lowered some barriers. Inequality within and between countries remained politically explosive. The 2008 financial crisis showed global contagion.
