Technological Advances: Debates About the Environment After 1900

Learning objectives

  • Explain the causes and effects of environmental changes in the period from 1900 to present.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Fossil-fuel energy, deforestation, and industrial agriculture drove climate change, biodiversity loss, and pollution. Debates pit growth and development against sustainability. The Club of Rome, Earth Day, and later IPCC reports shaped public argument. International agreements (Kyoto, Paris) have been unevenly implemented.

Water scarcity, urban air pollution, and oil spills became political. Developing countries argued that industrial latecomers should not bear equal cuts with historic high emitters — a justice debate inside environmental politics.
