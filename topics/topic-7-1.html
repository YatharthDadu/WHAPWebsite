Shifting Power After 1900

Learning objectives

  • Explain how internal and external factors contributed to change in various states after 1900.
Progress 0% · Score 0

The West’s industrial lead was challenged by the United States, Germany, and Japan. Land empires — Ottoman, Russian, Qing — faced internal reform crises and external pressure. The 1911 Revolution ended the Qing; the Ottoman Empire was the ‘sick man’ losing Balkan territory; Russia exploded in 1905 then 1917.

Mexico’s revolution (1910) showed that even formally independent states could be convulsed by inequality and foreign economic control. A global order still looked European — until World War I shattered it.
