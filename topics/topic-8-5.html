Decolonization After 1900

Learning objectives

  • Compare the processes by which various peoples pursued independence after 1900.
Progress 0% · Score 0
  • India and Pakistan (1947) — mass movement, partition violence; Gandhi’s nonviolence alongside other strategies.
  • Indonesia — war then independence from the Dutch.
  • Algeria — brutal war against France (1954–62).
  • Ghana — Nkrumah and a relatively negotiated British transfer.
  • Vietnam — war against France then the U.S.-backed south.
  • Kenya — Mau Mau emergency.
  • Portuguese Africa fought into the 1970s.

Some transfers were relatively peaceful; others were long wars. Cold War patronage shaped outcomes. Borders often followed colonial lines (uti possidetis), planting later conflicts.
