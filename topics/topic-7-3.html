Topic 7.3

Conducting World War I

Learning objectives

  • Explain how governments used a variety of methods to conduct war.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Industrial weapons — machine guns, artillery, poison gas, tanks, submarines, aircraft — produced trench stalemate on the Western Front and enormous casualties. Governments practiced total war: conscription, propaganda, censorship, economic planning, and targeting civilians (blockades).

Colonial troops fought in multiple theaters. Women entered factories and support roles. The United States entered in 1917; Russia exited after revolution. The armistice of November 1918 left Germany defeated but occupied only later in a different way — a bitter peace followed.
