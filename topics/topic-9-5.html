Calls for Reform and Responses After 1900

Learning objectives

  • Explain how social categories, roles, and practices have been maintained and challenged over time.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Women’s suffrage spread across the 20th century; later feminism addressed work, reproduction, and violence. Civil rights, anti-apartheid, LGBTQ+, and indigenous movements challenged legal discrimination. The UN Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948) set a global language even when states violated it.

Caste discrimination in India, racial hierarchies in the Americas and Africa, and religious minorities’ status remained contested. Some states expanded welfare; others retrenched. Progress was real and unfinished.
