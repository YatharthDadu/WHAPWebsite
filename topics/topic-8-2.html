Topic 8.2

The Cold War

Learning objectives

  • Explain the causes and effects of the ideological struggle of the Cold War.
Progress 0% · Score 0

U.S. containment (Truman Doctrine, Marshall Plan, NATO) met Soviet control of Eastern Europe (Warsaw Pact, 1955). The Chinese Communist victory (1949) and Korean War (1950–53) globalized the conflict. Berlin crises, the Hungarian uprising (1956), and the Cuban Missile Crisis (1962) were flashpoints.

Both sides used propaganda, espionage, athletic and space competition (Sputnik), and aid to allies. Non-aligned leaders (Nasser, Nehru, Tito, later Nkrumah) tried to avoid choosing a camp.
