Topic 4.7

Changing Social Hierarchies from 1450 to 1750

Learning objectives

  • Explain how social categories, roles, and practices have been maintained or have changed over time.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Spanish America developed casta hierarchies mixing Spanish, indigenous, and African ancestry (peninsulares, criollos, mestizos, mulatos). In the Ottoman world, women of the imperial household could wield influence (sultanate of women) while Islamic law structured family life. Qing policies distinguished Manchu conquerors from Han subjects (e.g., queue order).

European societies still ranked nobles, clergy, and commoners; some merchants gained wealth without full political equality. Expulsion and restriction of Jews continued in parts of Europe even as Sephardic diasporas flourished commercially. Elite women faced patriarchal limits but could be patrons, rulers, or cultural brokers.
