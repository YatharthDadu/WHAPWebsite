Britain combined coal and iron deposits, navigable rivers, capital from commerce and agriculture, protection of property, colonies and markets, and a culture of mechanical tinkering. The textile industry mechanized (spinning jenny, water frame, power loom); the steam engine (Watt) freed factories from waterpower sites.

Enclosure and agricultural productivity released labor. Urbanization and factory discipline created a new working class. This was a shift from organic energy (muscle, wood, water) toward fossil fuels.
