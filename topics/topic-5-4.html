Topic 5.4

Industrialization Spreads in the Period from 1750 to 1900

Learning objectives

  • Explain how different modes and locations of production have developed and changed over time.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Industrialization spread to western Europe, the United States, Russia (later, state-driven), and Japan (Meiji). Some regions became suppliers of raw materials and food (India after deindustrialization of textiles, Egypt cotton, Latin American exports) in an emerging global division of labor.

Steamships and railroads lowered transport costs, integrating markets. States that industrialize gained military power; those that did not faced new vulnerability (a bridge to Unit 6 imperialism).
