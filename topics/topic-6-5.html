Economic Imperialism from 1750 to 1900

Learning objectives

  • Explain how various economic factors contributed to the development of the global economy from 1750 to 1900.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Economic imperialism meant using trade, debt, and force to dominate without always annexing. Britain’s opium exports to China, the Opium Wars, and unequal treaties opened ports and granted extraterritoriality. Latin American states, politically independent, still faced British and later U.S. economic influence (railroads, loans, fruit and mineral companies).

Egypt’s cotton boom and debt led toward British occupation (1882). Sphere-of-influence imperialism in China is a core comparison with formal colonies in Africa.
