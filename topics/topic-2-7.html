Topic 2.7

Comparison of Economic Exchange

Learning objectives

  • Explain the similarities and differences among the various networks of exchange in the period from c. 1200 to c. 1450.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Silk Roads, Indian Ocean, and trans-Saharan routes all expanded because of improved transport, commercial practices, and powerful states. All carried religions and technologies as well as goods. All created wealthy cities and merchant diasporas.

Differences

  • Silk Roads: luxury goods, camels/horses, oasis cities, Mongol security.
  • Indian Ocean: bulk + luxury, monsoons, ships, port city-states.
  • Trans-Saharan: gold-salt, camels, Sudanic empires, Islamic scholarship in the Sahel.

None was ‘global’ in the post-1492 sense: the Americas remained separate. Comparison questions on the exam want specific goods, technologies, and states — not vague ‘they all traded.’
