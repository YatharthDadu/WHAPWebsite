Unresolved Tensions After World War I

Learning objectives

  • Explain the continuities and changes in territorial holdings from 1900 to the present.
Progress 0% · Score 0

The Treaty of Versailles blamed Germany, limited its army, and imposed reparations. New nation-states appeared in Eastern Europe from collapsed empires. The League of Nations was weak without the U.S. Ottoman Arab lands became League mandates under Britain and France — imperialism in new legal clothing, frustrating Arab nationalism. The Balfour Declaration complicated Palestine.

Japan and Italy felt cheated. German resentment and the myth of a ‘stab in the back’ fed the radical right. Anti-colonial movements (India’s Congress, Egypt, Korea 1919) used Wilsonian language of self-determination that victors did not apply equally.
