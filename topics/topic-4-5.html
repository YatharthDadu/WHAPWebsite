Topic 4.5

Maritime Empires Maintained and Developed

Learning objectives

  • Explain the continuities and changes in economic systems and labor systems from 1450 to 1750.
  • Explain changes and continuities in systems of slavery in the period from 1450 to 1750.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Mercantilist theory treated colonies as sources of bullion and exclusive markets. Silver from Potosí and Japan flowed into a truly global money system, much of it ending in China. The Atlantic system linked Europe, Africa, and the Americas: manufactured goods, enslaved Africans, and plantation sugar/tobacco/silver.

Labor systems

  • Chattel slavery in the Americas — hereditary, racialized, plantation-centered.
  • Encomienda, hacienda, and adapted mit’a in Spanish America.
  • Indentured servitude.
  • Continuities of slavery in African and Mediterranean societies, now connected to a massive Atlantic trade.
