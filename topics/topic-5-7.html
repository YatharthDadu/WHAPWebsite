Economic Developments and Innovations in the Industrial Age

Learning objectives

Progress 0% · Score 0

Limited-liability corporations, stock markets, and large banks mobilized capital. Gold-standard finance and transoceanic cables linked prices. Advertising and department stores appeared in industrial cities. Global export economies specialized (Chile nitrates, Brazil coffee, South African gold/diamonds, Indian cotton/tea).

Theories: Adam Smith’s critique of mercantilism and later classical economics; by the late century, monopolies and cartels prompted debate about regulation.
