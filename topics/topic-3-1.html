Topic 3.1

Empires Expand

Learning objectives

  • Explain how and why various land-based empires developed and expanded from 1450 to 1750.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Land-based empires expanded using gunpowder weapons, organized infantry, and artillery. The Ottoman, Safavid, and Mughal states (the ‘gunpowder empires’) plus Qing China, Russia, and others conquered vast territories.

Illustrative examples

  • Ottomans — captured Constantinople (1453); expanded into the Balkans, Middle East, and North Africa.
  • Safavids — Shi’a Iranian empire rivaling Ottomans.
  • Mughals — Muslim dynasty ruling much of Hindu India (Babur, Akbar).
  • Qing — Manchu conquest of Ming China.
  • Russia — eastward expansion across Siberia; later south toward warm seas.
  • Songhai — West African expansion (until Moroccan guns, 1591).
