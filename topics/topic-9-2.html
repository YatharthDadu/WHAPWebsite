Technological Advances and Limitations After 1900: Disease

Learning objectives

  • Explain how environmental factors affected human populations over time.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Medical science reduced deaths from smallpox (eradicated), polio, and many bacterial infections. Yet malaria, tuberculosis, and HIV/AIDS killed millions, especially where poverty limited treatment. Influenza (1918) and later COVID-19 showed that connected worlds spread pathogens quickly.

Diseases of longevity (heart disease, diabetes, cancer) rose where people lived longer and diets changed. Epidemic response became a global political issue (WHO, patent debates over medicines).
