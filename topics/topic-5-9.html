Topic 5.9

Society and the Industrial Age

Learning objectives

  • Explain how industrialization caused change in existing social hierarchies and standards of living.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Industrial middle classes (managers, professionals, clerks) grew. A working class crowded into unsanitary cities; over time, public health, diet, and real wages improved for many in the industrial core — after terrible early decades. The ‘cult of domesticity’ idealized middle-class women’s home role even as working-class women and children labored in mills.

New leisure (sports, music halls), public education, and consumer goods appeared. Inequality remained stark. Rural life changed more slowly. Racial and gender hierarchies structured who received the gains of growth.
