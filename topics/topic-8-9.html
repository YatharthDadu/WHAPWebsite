Learning objectives

  • Explain the extent to which the effects of the Cold War were similar in the Eastern and Western Hemispheres.

The Cold War structured alliances, aid, and wars on every inhabited continent, but local social conflicts, religion, and anti-colonial politics were not mere puppets. Decolonization would have happened without superpower rivalry — yet the rivalry shaped who got weapons and which regimes survived. Compare Latin American coups, African proxy wars, and Asian hot wars to European division: same global conflict, different intensity.
