Columbian Exchange

Learning objectives

  • Explain the causes of the Columbian Exchange and its effects on the Eastern and Western Hemispheres.
Progress 0% · Score 0

The Columbian Exchange transferred plants, animals, people, and diseases between hemispheres. American maize, potatoes, and cassava later fueled population growth in Afro-Eurasia. Afro-Eurasian wheat, rice, sugar, horses, cattle, and pigs transformed American landscapes and transport.

Disease (smallpox, measles, influenza) devastated indigenous populations who lacked immunity — a primary cause of social collapse and colonial conquest. Enslaved Africans were forcibly brought to the Americas, especially for sugar. Cash-crop monocultures reshaped environments (deforestation, soil exhaustion).
