Topic 7.4

Economy in the Interwar Period

Learning objectives

  • Explain how different governments responded to economic crisis after 1900.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Wartime debt, German reparations struggles, and the 1929 crash produced a global Great Depression. Governments responded differently: U.S. New Deal regulation and relief; Scandinavian social democracy; Soviet Five-Year Plans (already underway) using state command and brutal collectivization; fascist Italy and Nazi Germany combining public works, rearmament, and dictatorship.

Export economies in Latin America and colonies suffered collapsing commodity prices. Economic crisis delegitimized liberal democracy in some countries and empowered authoritarians.
