Topic 9.7

Resistance to Globalization After 1900

Learning objectives

  • Explain the various reactions to existing globalization processes from 1900 to present.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Labor unions, environmentalists, and Global South activists protested IMF/World Bank conditionality and WTO rounds (Seattle 1999). Populist nationalists later attacked migration, trade deals, and supranational institutions (Brexit, various anti-EU and anti-immigrant parties). Religious fundamentalisms rejected some secular consumer culture.

Resistance is not one movement: the left may critique inequality and ecology; the right may critique borders and identity. Both respond to real dislocations of global capitalism and cultural change.
