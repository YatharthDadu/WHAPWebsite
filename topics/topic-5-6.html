Industrialization: Government’s Role from 1750 to 1900

Learning objectives

  • Explain the causes and effects of economic strategies of different states and empires.
Progress 0% · Score 0
  • Meiji Restoration — dismantled samurai order, imported experts, built railways, created conglomerates, reformed army and schools.
  • Muhammad Ali in Egypt — cotton, factories, and military modernization under Ottoman nominal rule.
  • Tsarist Russia — state railways (Trans-Siberian), later Witte’s policies.
  • Ottoman Tanzimat — legal and military reform under European pressure.
  • Qing Self-Strengthening — limited industrial/military reform that did not transform the state fully.

Some states used tariffs and state investment; Britain leaned more on early market advantage and empire. Reform was often a response to military defeat or unequal treaties.
