Technology of the Industrial Age

Learning objectives

  • Explain how technology shaped economic production over time.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Steel (Bessemer), chemicals, electricity, and petroleum joined coal and steam. Telegraph and later telephone compressed information. Internal combustion and electricity powered new cities and armies. Medical and chemical advances changed daily life and war.

Fossil fuels multiplied energy available per worker. The gap between industrial and non-industrial regions widened — a key cause of the ‘new imperialism.’
