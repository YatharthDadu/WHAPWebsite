Nationalism and Revolutions in the Period from 1750 to 1900

Learning objectives

  • Explain causes and effects of the various revolutions in the period from 1750 to 1900.
Progress 0% · Score 0
  • American Revolution — colonial tax/representation conflict; republic with limited franchise.
  • French Revolution — fiscal crisis, estates, then republic, Terror, and Napoleon; Declaration of the Rights of Man.
  • Haitian Revolution — enslaved people overthrew French colonial slavery and created an independent Black republic (Toussaint Louverture; 1804 independence).
  • Spanish American wars — creole-led independence (Bolívar, San Martín) that often preserved social hierarchy.

Nationalism imagined shared language, history, and territory as the basis of the state. It unified Germany and Italy, threatened multiethnic empires (Ottoman, Habsburg, Russian), and inspired anti-colonial thought. New nation-states issued constitutions, flags, and schools that taught loyalty.
