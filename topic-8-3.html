Topic 8.3

Effects of the Cold War

Learning objectives

  • Compare the ways in which the United States and the Soviet Union sought to maintain influence over the course of the Cold War.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Korea, Vietnam, Afghanistan (1979–89), Angola, Nicaragua, and other conflicts were Cold War battlegrounds. Superpowers armed clients, staged coups, and sometimes intervened directly. Nuclear arms racing (MAD) coexisted with arms-control talks (later SALT).

Domestic life was shaped too: McCarthyism in the U.S.; Soviet dissent crushed (Prague Spring 1968). The space race produced satellites used for both prestige and surveillance. Newly independent states could bargain for aid — or be destablized.
