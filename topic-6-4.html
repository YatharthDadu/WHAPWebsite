Topic 6.4

Global Economic Development from 1750 to 1900

Learning objectives

  • Explain how various environmental factors contributed to the development of the global economy from 1750 to 1900.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Industrial demand created export booms: cotton, rubber, palm oil, guano, meat, wheat, copper, gold, diamonds. Railroads and steamships tied interiors to ports. Plantations and mines reshaped landscapes, often using coerced or semi-coerced labor.

Environmental consequences included deforestation, soil exhaustion, and monoculture risk. Profits frequently flowed to foreign investors and local elites more than to workers.
