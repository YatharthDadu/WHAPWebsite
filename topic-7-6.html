Causes of World War II

Learning objectives

  • Explain the causes and consequences of World War II.
Progress 0% · Score 0

The Nazi seizure of power, Japanese expansion in Manchuria and China, and Italian aggression in Ethiopia tested the League. Hitler rebuilt the military, remilitarized the Rhineland, annexed Austria, and took the Sudetenland (Munich 1938). Appeasement failed. The Nazi-Soviet Pact (1939) cleared the way to invade Poland.

Causes combined unresolved WWI settlements, the Depression, aggressive ideologies (fascism, militarism), and the failure of collective security. In Asia, Japan’s imperial war in China (from 1937) was already World War II’s opening for millions.
