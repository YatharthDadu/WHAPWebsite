Topic 8.8

End of the Cold War

Learning objectives

  • Explain the causes of the end of the Cold War.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Soviet economic stagnation, the costly Afghan war, the arms race, and nationalist unrest in the USSR and Eastern Europe wore down the system. Gorbachev’s glasnost and perestroika opened criticism without fixing the economy. Eastern European revolutions in 1989 toppled communist regimes; the Berlin Wall fell. The Soviet Union dissolved in 1991.

U.S. military spending and diplomacy (Nixon’s earlier China opening; Reagan’s later pressure) mattered, but internal Soviet crisis was decisive. The unipolar moment was brief; new conflicts followed.
