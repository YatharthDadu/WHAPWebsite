Topic 3.3

Empires: Belief Systems

Learning objectives

  • Explain continuity and change within the various belief systems during the period from 1450 to 1750.
Progress 0% · Score 0

Martin Luther’s challenge (1517) to indulgences and papal authority split western Christianity. Protestant traditions (Lutheran, Calvinist, Anglican) multiplied. The Catholic Reformation (Council of Trent, Jesuits, Index) reaffirmed doctrine while reforming some abuses. Wars of religion followed (French Wars of Religion, Thirty Years’ War).

  • Ottoman Sunni vs. Safavid Shi’a rivalry politicized sectarian identity.
  • Akbar’s relatively inclusive policies vs. later Mughal orthodoxy under Aurangzeb.
  • Sikhism emerged in South Asia, blending elements in a distinct monotheistic community.
  • In the Americas, Christianity spread under colonial states, often mixing with indigenous practices — a preview of Unit 4.

Belief systems both unified empires and generated resistance. Printing helped Protestant ideas travel; new schools and missions expanded literacy in some regions.
