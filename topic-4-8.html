Continuity and Change from 1450 to 1750

Learning objectives

  • Explain how economic developments from 1450 to 1750 affected social structures over time.
Progress 0% · Score 0

The world became hemispherically connected. Silver, sugar, and enslaved labor created an Atlantic system. Land-based empires still dominated much of Eurasia. Peasant agriculture remained the majority experience. Religions adapted rather than vanished.

New: global commodity chains, racialized slavery in the Americas, European maritime empires. Old: tribute, peasant labor, elite landholding, patriarchal households, and powerful Asian economies (China and India still central to global manufacturing).
